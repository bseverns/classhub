**Mission:** Build a safer LED circuit and explain what the resistor is doing for the system.

## Build
- Start with one low-voltage power source, one LED, and one resistor.
- Build the circuit and test it.
- Add simple labels that show LED, resistor, and power source.

## Reflection
- Finish the sentence: "The resistor helps by ___."
- Take a clear photo that shows the lit LED and the labels.

## Teacher prep
- Build one sample that works and one sample with the LED reversed.
- Pre-sort resistor bins so students do not spend class searching for tiny parts.
- Remind students that this is low-voltage classroom circuitry only.

## Materials
- LEDs
- resistors
- batteries or low-voltage power packs
- breadboards or snap-circuit parts
- jumper wires

## Agenda
- 0-10 min: Name the new parts and show the sample.
- 10-20 min: Demo of LED direction and resistor placement.
- 20-42 min: Student build and troubleshoot.
- 42-52 min: Add labels and explain the parts.
- 52-60 min: Photograph and upload artifact.

## Checkpoints
- LED lights in a complete circuit.
- Resistor is included in the path.
- Student names each main part.

## Common stuck points + fixes
- "The circuit worked before I moved it."
  - Check each wire and rebuild the smallest loop.
- "I forgot which part is the resistor."
  - Compare by size and color bands, then point to it before testing.

## Extensions
- Add a second LED if materials allow.
- Compare which labels make the clearest explanation for another student.
