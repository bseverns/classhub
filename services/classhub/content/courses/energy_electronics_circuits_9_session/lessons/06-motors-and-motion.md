**Mission:** Make something move, then decide whether your next fix belongs in the circuit or in the structure.

## Build
- Start with a simple motor circuit.
- Add one small moving attachment made from lightweight craft materials.
- Test the build, then add a switch if time allows.

## Reflection
- Explain what part creates the motion and what part controls it.
- Record a short video that shows the moving build.

## Teacher prep
- Build one motor sample with a lightweight paper attachment.
- Pre-cut simple craft parts so setup time stays short.
- Remind students to turn power off before changing wiring.

## Materials
- motors
- low-voltage power packs
- switches or pushbuttons
- craft materials
- Chromebooks

## Agenda
- 0-10 min: Demo motor output and safety reminder.
- 10-20 min: Build the simplest motion loop.
- 20-42 min: Add lightweight mechanism or decoration.
- 42-50 min: Troubleshoot circuit versus structure.
- 50-60 min: Record artifact and explain the motion.

## Checkpoints
- Motor runs.
- Student can identify one control or support part.
- Video or photo shows the result clearly.

## Common stuck points + fixes
- "Nothing moves."
  - Remove the attachment and test the motor by itself.
- "The motor spins but the project does not."
  - Fix the connection between motor shaft and moving piece.

## Extensions
- Add a second motion pattern such as spin versus wobble.
- Build a sign that tells another student how to use the switch.
