**Mission:** Show what you can build, record it clearly, and explain how it works using real parts language.

## Build
- Choose one build from the course to improve, repair, or combine.
- Make sure one feature works on purpose.
- Prepare one photo or short video and one short explanation.

## Reflection
- Use this frame: "This part is ___. The path goes ___. The function is ___."
- Upload your artifact with the class naming routine.

## Teacher prep
- Set out mixed parts from earlier sessions in clearly labeled bins.
- Prepare one example of a good final artifact that shows project only.
- Keep the focus on documentation, not perfection.

## Materials
- low-voltage classroom parts from earlier sessions
- Chromebooks
- reflection sheet

## Agenda
- 0-10 min: Review course highlights and final goal.
- 10-35 min: Build, improve, or repair.
- 35-48 min: Practice explanation with partner.
- 48-58 min: Record artifact and upload.
- 58-60 min: Quick closing share.

## Checkpoints
- One feature works or is clearly documented.
- Student explanation includes part, path, and function.
- Artifact is uploaded.

## Common stuck points + fixes
- "My big idea is not finished."
  - Capture the strongest working piece and explain that clearly.
- "I do not know what to say."
  - Point to one part, trace one path, then name the result.

## Extensions
- Add labels or arrows to your final photo.
- Leave one kind suggestion for a partner's next version.
