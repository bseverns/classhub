**Mission:** Add a control part that lets you decide when the circuit is open and when it is closed.

## Build
- Start with a simple working circuit from Session 3.
- Add a switch or pushbutton into the path.
- Test whether you can repeat the same on/off behavior three times in a row.

## Reflection
- Say: "When the switch is ___, the path is ___."
- Record a short clip or take a photo of the controlled build.

## Teacher prep
- Test each switch type ahead of class so students can compare parts.
- Prepare one example that visibly fails when the switch is placed in the wrong spot.
- Keep a small bin of spare buttons and jumper wires ready.

## Materials
- switches and pushbuttons
- LEDs or buzzers
- breadboards or snap-circuit parts
- jumper wires
- Chromebooks

## Agenda
- 0-8 min: Review loop idea from last session.
- 8-15 min: Teacher demo with open and closed states.
- 15-40 min: Student build and repeated testing.
- 40-50 min: Explain the path with pointing and talk.
- 50-60 min: Artifact capture and upload.

## Checkpoints
- Build turns on and off on purpose.
- Student uses open and closed correctly.
- Artifact clearly shows the control part.

## Common stuck points + fixes
- "The switch is there, but nothing changes."
  - Trace the path and move the switch into the main loop.
- "It worked once, then stopped."
  - Reseat the switch and return to the simplest working version.

## Extensions
- Try both a toggle switch and a pushbutton and compare the feel.
- Create a simple sign that tells another student how to use your control.
