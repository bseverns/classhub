ClassHub stores a learner's chosen display name, class submissions and responses, and basic activity times. Student email and passwords are not required. The optional AI Homework Helper temporarily keeps recent redacted student/tutor turns in the server cache for up to two hours so follow-up questions make sense; routine conversation archives are off by default.

AI help is optional. A learner may participate without using it by asking the facilitator for the same lesson support. The helper is designed to coach rather than complete graded work. Safety disclosures pause tutoring and direct the learner to a trusted adult or emergency help when danger is immediate.

Uploads are private unless a gallery item is deliberately published with the class's consent process. Gallery consent choice: ☐ may share approved work  ☐ keep work private.

Submissions and class events follow the program's posted retention schedule. Learners can review/download data and request or perform deletion at `/student/my-data`; immediate deletion also clears their transient helper context. Any explicitly enabled operator archive follows a separate restricted retention process and cannot guarantee per-student removal from historical snapshot files.
