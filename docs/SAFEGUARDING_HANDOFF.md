## Staff response

When a learner discloses harm, danger, abuse, self-harm, or feeling unsafe:

1. Pause tutoring or ordinary troubleshooting and stay with the learner when it is safe to do so.
2. Listen without investigating, leading questions, or promising confidentiality.
3. Record only the minimum factual information required by local policy: the learner's words, date/time, staff member, and action taken.
4. Contact the primary safeguarding lead immediately; use the backup if the primary cannot be reached.
5. The named reporting owner follows the applicable local emergency and mandated-reporting process. ClassHub does not decide whether a legal report is required.
6. Restrict notes to authorized recipients and the approved retention location; do not paste the disclosure into Helper or general chat tools.
7. Record completion of the handoff without adding sensitive disclosure text to ClassHub audit metadata.

For immediate danger, staff follow the filled local emergency process above before routine internal escalation.
