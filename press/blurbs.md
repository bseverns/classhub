## 140-word
Class Hub is a self-hosted micro-LMS designed for real classroom operations and low-surveillance teaching. Students use a low-friction join flow (class code + pseudonym display name), land on a multi-lingual weekly launch page, and can rename/export/delete from `/student/my-data`. Artifact sharing is teacher-only by default; students can opt in per submission, and teachers moderate gallery visibility. Teachers also get a help-first facilitation board that prioritizes “I’m stuck” signals and recent upload friction without ranking students. The platform includes invite-only cohorts with expiring/seat-capped links, organization-aware staff access, outcomes/certificate workflows, and per-class retention presets applied by existing prune jobs. Architecture stays intentionally inspectable: Django services behind Caddy with Postgres and Redis. A separate Homework Helper provides lesson-scoped hints and avoids prompt archiving. Operational runbooks and CI checks support reliable self-hosted delivery.
